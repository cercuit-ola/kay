I'm Kay — an entrepreneur, founder, and impact-driven leader building at the intersection of Travel · Education · Media · Financial Freedom · Global Impact.

After paying off over $200,000 in debt, I chose a life centered around freedom, travel, and intentional living — traveling across 70+ countries with my son while building Innovasol, a multi-division ecosystem including Rooted Journeys, media platforms, worldschooling programs, and community impact initiatives.

My mission: empower families worldwide to break out of traditional systems and design lives rooted in purpose, flexibility, and global exposure.

A life lived across continents — with intention.
