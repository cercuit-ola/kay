Who We Are

We Are Innovasol

A global media, travel, and brand experience company building stories and experiences that connect cultures worldwide.

Our Story

Innovasol Enterprise is a global media, travel, and brand experience company headquartered in the United States with teams across North America and Africa.

We create and manage brands that blend storytelling, strategy, travel, culture, and impact to inspire global audiences and build meaningful human connections.

Through innovative media, immersive experiences, and community-centered initiatives, we help people explore the world, connect across cultures, and create lasting change.

We build and manage purpose-driven brands across media, travel, lifestyle, and social impact. Our work combines creativity, strategy, and global experiences to educate, inspire, and empower communities worldwide.

From digital storytelling and creator-led campaigns to curated travel experiences and philanthropic initiatives, Innovasol transforms ideas into experiences that connect people globally.

"Inspiring connection, creativity, and change through strategy, storytelling, and experiences."

Our Four Divisions

Innovasol Enterprise operates through four specialized divisions, each focused on a distinct area of influence and impact.

01
Lifestyle & Influence Division

Innovasol
Collective

Innovasol Collective is a global lifestyle and influence division focused on intentional living, family travel, culture, and global connection.

Through storytelling, curated experiences, and community engagement, the division inspires families to explore the world, embrace cultural learning, and raise globally minded citizens.

Travel & Experiences Division

Innovasol
Travel

Innovasol Travel is the core execution and experience division of Innovasol Enterprise, designing and managing immersive, purpose-driven travel experiences that educate, inspire, and connect communities around the world.
